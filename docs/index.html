October 17, 2026

Denver Reported Offenses

Denver offense reports fell 18.7 percent from the 2022 peak to 2025, and the entire net decline is vehicle offenses: vehicle reports fell by half while reports not involving a vehicle climbed 11.2 percent to their highest level of six matched windows; the 2026 window ticked up 1.6 percent, the first increase since the peak.

Denver Property Sales

Denver recorded 105,585 market sales from 2020 to October 2026: the median price rose from $450,000 in 2020 to a $595,000 peak in 2022 and has held between $525,000 and $550,000 since, and the share of sales at or under $500,000 fell from 57.2 percent to 47.4 percent.

Denver Net Housing Change

Denver permitted 11,585 new residential buildings and 3,837 demolitions from January 2020 to October 2026, a net of 7,748 structures; 60 percent of demolitions were followed by a new-building permit at the same address, and 11 neighborhoods demolished more than they built.

Denver Homelessness

Denver spends roughly $105,000 per long-term housing exit (about $178.1M against roughly 1,700 exits): a 43% housing-exit rate for people brought indoors, and shelter beds at roughly $34,000-$36,000 per bed per year, on par with permanent housing.
